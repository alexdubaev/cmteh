# Business compatibility discovery — 2026-11-11

Scope: current source `5b09079`, OpenCart 4.1.0.4 plus CMTEH headless storefront. No product implementation or production operation in this discovery.
Decision: connect existing site entities through native admin/session/ACL; do not manufacture customers, subscribers, orders, revenue or sales stages from enquiries. Report unsupported commerce capabilities separately.
Native admin users and groups are already useful manager tools. Public customer accounts are a different, currently missing site capability; reorganizing admin needs no new authentication.
Contact registry must retain source type and public request/quote ID. Equal email/phone is not proof that two submissions are one verified customer; expose source records rather than silently merging identities.

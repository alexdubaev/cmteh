## Result and dependency boundary

Account-independent newsletter signup, confirmation, unsubscribe, suppression, a consent-aware campaign outbox, consent preferences, and prospective source capture are independent projects. They can operate for guests and must not create `customer`, `order`, `customer_affiliate`, commission, or subscription records from RFQs. Existing lead/quote transactional mail remains its own process. Tracking consent and email-marketing consent are separate purposes and grants; neither implies the other.

Existing campaign CRUD and permissions are useful. Existing newsletter/contact send, coupon calculation and affiliate accounting are not drop-in independent services: they consume native customer/session/cart/order state. Analytics can also be independent, but new collection must precede truthful reports. No migration can recover historical first/last touches or consent from caller-declared `page_url`.

## Withdrawal, race safety, and outbox truthfulness

Application dedupe and no-send-after-committed-unsubscribe require more than selecting a confirmed list once:

1. Start a frozen campaign once under request-key uniqueness; audience is only confirmed grants for its purpose, with no suppression. Materialize per-recipient jobs with consent generation; never select all customers/leads. Deduplication is a DB constraint, not browser button disabling.
2. Subscription confirm/unsubscribe/suppress, campaign enqueue and worker dispatch must use one consistent lock order per canonical address/purpose. The final dispatch reads current revision/state and suppression from the **primary DB after acquiring the lock**, not a lagging replica or stale audience cache.
3. Unsubscribe atomically marks state withdrawn, increments revision, revokes pending confirmation tokens, records evidence, and cancels pending/retry jobs. Worker must recheck every job after claim and before handoff; old revision, suppression, paused campaign or expired grant cancels it.
4. A check followed by unlocked network send still races. Serialize final eligibility check **and bounded MTA handoff** against withdrawal with the same per-recipient dispatch lock, also acquired by unsubscribe/suppress. A short DB advisory lock can extend over network send without a long row transaction; all writers must participate and connection/lock failure must stop sending. Withdrawal may wait for the bounded already-started handoff. Define and expose the invariant precisely: no new MTA handoff starts after withdrawal has committed. Mail already accepted by an MTA cannot be recalled and can arrive later. Never promise otherwise.
5. Persist attempt/lease before transport; use stable delivery ID and Message-ID. On transport success, record acceptance once. A crash after SMTP acceptance and before DB acknowledgement creates **unknown delivery**, not a proven failure. Plain SMTP/PHP mail offers no application exactly-once guarantee. For marketing, hold an uncertain job for investigation rather than automatically replaying ambiguous acceptance; definite pre-handoff failures may retry within configured policy. If a selected transport later provides documented idempotency, use that stable ID; provider selection and delivery volume remain owner decisions.
6. Re-consent requires fresh intentional request and confirmation, new generation and audit event; it never revives cancelled old-campaign jobs. Suppression precedence, hard-bounce/complaint sources and authorized removal require explicit rules. Keep transactional RFQ routing separate; withdrawing marketing does not mutate RFQ snapshots or worker states.

No current provider feedback endpoint exists in the examined C surface. Add authenticated, replay-safe feedback only for the actual selected transport; do not invent webhook trust or label `mail()` acceptance as delivered/read. Campaign pause/cancel, duplicate start, permanent rejection and uncertain delivery counts need admin visibility. Open/click pixels are additional tracking, not part of the minimal mail core.

## Prospective consent and trustworthy source capture

Use a separate `TrackingConsent` / `SourceCapture` service with a policy-versioned consent event and purpose flags. Suggested internal contracts: `captureLanding(array $trustedRequest, ConsentContext $consent): ?CaptureProof`, `resolveForSubmission(string $cookie, ConsentContext $consent): ?SourceSnapshot`; receipt and proof issuance are server-side. These do not imply an authenticated customer.

- No optional visitor/attribution cookie, local storage ID or raw event persistence before the required purpose grant. The necessary preference receipt is distinct, minimal and not reused as a hidden analytics ID. Rejection/withdrawal must work without losing catalog, filters, search, request list or RFQ submission. No consent means honest `unknown`, with no retroactive reconstruction from logs or old contacts.
- On a granted current navigation, parse a small allowlist of tracking/UTM keys with strict scalar/length/encoding rules, resolve native campaign code on the server, and issue a new opaque capture ID. Save server time, canonical allowed landing path, sanitized allowlisted source fields, native ID plus code/name snapshot, consent revision and capture method. Do not persist arbitrary query strings, URL credentials/fragments, full referrer paths or token endpoints. Referrer is untrusted; at most retain the approved normalized origin/category.
- First touch is immutable within the chosen attribution window/generation; last touch changes only according to approved eligible-touch rules. Use server-side row locking/version updates for simultaneous tabs, and an owner-selected expiry/reset/direct-visit policy. A new valid tracking code cannot be overwritten merely by an old native cookie. Preserve `tracking` legacy behavior only in its existing labelled report until a separately reviewed transition is implemented.
- Bind capture to a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite cookie (new name, e.g. `cmteh_source`; path and TTL explicit). If stateless proof is used, sign capture ID/purpose/revision/server timestamps/key ID with **`CMTEH_ATTRIBUTION_TOKEN_KEY`** (variable name only), bound to a server record and expiry. Rotate/revoke on withdrawal; do not share the email-marketing key. The submit router resolves the server-issued cookie/proof; it does not accept a posted campaign ID or `page_url` as evidence.
- Proof means “our server observed and recorded these declared landing parameters with this consent,” not proof of an ad impression, unique person, honest referrer, or non-bot. Public campaign links can be forged/copied. Keep capture confidence/method explicit; do not overclaim fraud prevention.
- Add an internal optional source context to the lead/quote persistence seam, outside client body normalization/fingerprint. Preferred bounded schema change: nullable source snapshot/version columns in the original lead/quote row, written in the same **first insert** as that accepted snapshot, with an attribution schema preflight and feature gate. Resolve/validate optional context before save; unavailable/withdrawn/invalid proof becomes null and never blocks ordinary RFQ validation. Replays return the original snapshot and cannot add/replace source. SQL persistence failure still follows existing atomic RFQ behavior. No extra call to native order/marketing reports on submit.
- Additive migrations leave existing source columns null. Alternative auxiliary association table requires a same-transaction insertion on first acceptance or an explicitly reported loss window; best-effort post-commit attachment must not later attach the replay's current campaign. The preferred nullable snapshot avoids that extra association write/race. This protected snapshot/schema change warrants real DB and existing RFQ acceptance gates, not a presentation-only release.
- Keep `page_url` unchanged as caller-declared evidence. Add separate verified-source reporting grouped by captured first/last policy/version and `unknown/invalid/withdrawn/not collected`; maintain per-submission uniqueness via actual lead/quote primary key. Count one accepted RFQ once; retries and notifications are not additional conversions. Revenue/order/partner payout metrics remain absent until their real event pipeline exists.

Proposed new tables: `cmteh_tracking_consent` (opaque receipt, revision, purposes/policy/time/withdrawal), `cmteh_source_capture` (opaque capture, consent revision, first/last snapshots, server issue/expiry and state), and bounded `cmteh_analytics_event` / daily aggregate tables only if the analytics feature is commissioned. UNIQUE event identity, purpose/indexed time window, retention job and restricted admin permissions are required. Immutable consent evidence and retained source snapshots need different retention/erasure decisions; do not delete proof by truncating all analytics or erase business documents by matching an email.

## Conditional coupons and partners

Coupons can initially be a read-only/native definition editor without public redemption; that is not “discounts working.” An RFQ may collect a requested promo code as a non-binding manager note only after owner chooses that behavior; no amount, reservation or history should be inferred. Real discount evaluation needs accepted prices/currency/rounding/tax/shipping/stacking/scopes, eligibility and redemption lifecycle. Once a commercial order exists, use native definitions if they fit and add transactional reservation/redemption uniqueness (`coupon_id,order_id,redemption_generation`), serialization for last-use races, expiry and reversal policy. No fabricated `order_id=0` or fake native customer for guest quotas.

Affiliates are optional and can be independent of shopper accounts only via a separate partner identity/onboarding contract. If owner deliberately chooses existing verified native customers as partners, reuse their editor with guarded mutations; otherwise do not force customer creation to satisfy `customer_affiliate` FK. Define partner approval and verified payout details, attribution conflict/anti-self-referral, earning event, rate/rounding/currency, pending/approved/reversed balances and payout authorization. A minimal audited ledger can key accrual by a real approved conversion/order and rules version; UNIQUE conversion+partner+generation plus compensating reversal prevents retries paying twice. Preserve original rule snapshots. No automatically earned commission per RFQ and no cash payout implementation/provider/bank integration is included in the core estimate.
